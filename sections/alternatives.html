Heyday Sun Prairie

Sun Prairie

Must tour

Why it made the list: Probably the cleanest Wisconsin equivalent to Redwood Volo, and one of the most important tours because it tests whether “luxury” is actually better as quiet, house-like living instead of a fancy lobby.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
1BR: $1,820 (832 sf) or $2,215 (1,076 sf). 2BR: $2,435 (1,344 sf) or $2,595 (1,457 sf). 3BR: pricing currently by inquiry.
Best budget fit: both 1BRs and the 1,344-sf 2BR. Pricing is especially compelling because the private 2-car garage is part of the housing concept.
  • Stories: single-story ranch-style homes only — no stairs in the unit and nobody above or below.
  • Construction/material: confirmed wood-frame construction on slab-on-grade foundations. Because it is single-story, floor/ceiling impact noise from another household is eliminated even though the framing is wood.
  • Parking: private direct-access 2-car garage with every home, plus a 22-foot parking apron/driveway; the property advertises private parking for up to 4 cars and EV charging capability in each garage.
  • Professional operation: apartment-style leasing and maintenance, including 24-hour maintenance requests.
  • Deliveries: each home has its own address; deliveries go directly to the door instead of a package room.
  • Setting: 41 acres with walking paths/open space; much less “city apartment” feeling.
  • Current promotion: the property is advertising up to 1.5 months free on select 2BRs — useful as context, but not something to count on for an April 2027 move.
  • Bottom line: one of the strongest combinations of price, privacy, quiet and low-hassle living on the entire list.

Residences at Oak View

Oak Creek

Lifestyle test

Why it made the list: Actual detached single-family rental homes inside one professionally managed community. This is the “what if I want a house without being a homeowner yet?” test.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
2BR floor plans have recently advertised around $2,500–$2,550. 3BRs are roughly $3,100–$3,400. No 1BR layouts.
Today’s visible inventory skews toward 3BRs, but that should not eliminate it: the community has 2BR Bella/Luca plans, and recent 2BR pricing sits right around the target.
  • Stories: detached 1- and 2-story single-family rental homes depending on floor plan.
  • Construction/material: detached single-family construction with zero shared walls. I have not found a reliable public source specifying the structural framing material, so this is one to ask directly on the tour rather than assume.
  • Parking: attached garage plus private/surface parking; the property explicitly advertises no parking or storage fees.
  • Detached: no upstairs neighbor, no downstairs neighbor and no shared walls at all.
  • No parking/storage fees: the property explicitly advertises both as included.
  • Amenities: heated pool, clubhouse, gym, business center, pickleball/basketball, dog park and dog wash.
  • Guest option: a furnished guest home is available for visiting friends/family.
  • Bottom line: a 2BR around $2.5k would be compelling; the 3BR pricing is probably more space than needed and more than the preferred spend.

The Carillon at Grafton

Grafton · opening expected 2027

Future tour

Why it made the list: Potentially the most interesting spring-2027 wildcard. It combines house-like layouts, yards and garages with professional rental operation — and its opening timeline lines up unusually well with an April 2027 move.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
No final leasing prices posted yet. During approvals, the developer targeted $1.80–$2.30 per sq. ft. on homes of roughly 1,250–1,850 sf.
That development-stage target implies roughly $2,250–$2,875 for a 1,250-sf home and $3,330–$4,255 for a 1,850-sf home. These are planning estimates, NOT announced rents.
  • Stories: a mix of single-story living and multi-level layouts with first-floor primary suites; only 34 homes across 10 buildings.
  • Construction/material: structural framing has not been clearly published in the documents I found. Exterior approvals allow LP engineered-wood siding or Hardie fiber-cement siding, with stone veneer accents on selected elevations.
  • Parking: every home gets an attached 1- or 2-car garage; project plans also include driveway/apron parking, with garages built directly into each residential structure.
  • Timing: completion/first occupancy is expected around spring 2027 — directly relevant to the April lease end.
  • Outdoor space: every home gets a private enclosed yard maintained by the community.
  • Budget reality: the smallest homes could plausibly land near the $2,500 target if final pricing is near the low end; larger homes likely will not.
  • Bottom line: keep it high on the watchlist until actual unit rents and model-home tours go live.

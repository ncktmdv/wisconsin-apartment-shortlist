The Couture

Downtown Milwaukee

Must tour

Why it made the list: The strongest “actual luxury” benchmark so far. Tour it even if downtown Milwaukee is not the preferred lifestyle, because it sets the quality/service ceiling for everything else.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
1BR: about $1,920–$2,665 base rent in the current market. 2BR: from about $3,605. 3BR: far above the target.
Best budget fit: smaller/junior 1BRs. A number of 1BR layouts can land at or under the $2,500 target depending on floor and timing.
  • Stories: 44-story residential tower (completed 2024).
  • Construction/material: concrete high-rise; cast-in-place concrete structural elements/slabs with extensive precast-concrete exterior components — a much stronger sound-isolation starting point than ordinary wood-frame apartments.
  • Parking: paid heated underground resident parking; current official rates are about $215/mo standard, $315/mo EV, or $300/mo tandem. Six designated residential guest spaces are in the above-grade public structure.
  • True full-service setup: 24/7 concierge/doorman rather than a leasing desk that closes at 5.
  • Noise: resident feedback we found specifically praised not hearing neighbors/outside noise; still worth testing in person.
  • Fees: official site lists a $95/mo utility package for 1BRs ($125 for 2BRs); no monthly amenity fee.
  • Lease flexibility: official FAQ lists terms from 3–18 months.
  • Bottom line: surprisingly attainable if a high-quality 1BR is enough; 2BRs are where pricing jumps hard.

7SEVENTY7

Downtown Milwaukee

Must tour

Why it made the list: The established full-service comparison to Couture. If the goal is “Upton-style service + quiet + professional operation,” this is one of the most relevant buildings in the state.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
Studio: about $1,899 base / $2,059 total monthly. 1BR: roughly $2,262–$2,512 base / $2,422–$2,672 total. 2BR: about $3,299 base / $3,459 total.
Best budget fit: studio or lower-priced 1BR. The site distinguishes base rent from the broader “Total Monthly Leasing Price,” which is the more useful comparison.
  • Stories: 35-story tower; residential floors sit above a large integrated parking podium/garage.
  • Construction/material: mostly cast-in-place concrete / all-concrete tower construction, with steel used for stairs and miscellaneous elements.
  • Parking: integrated 10-story garage with about 1,400 total stalls shared among residents, Northwestern Mutual and some public users; heated resident spaces and EV charging are available. Resident parking pricing varies/uses a third-party operator.
  • 24-hour Resident Experience Team: closer to hotel/residential-service staffing than normal apartment management.
  • Resident services: package and grocery delivery, valet-style services, resident guest suites and help coordinating outside services.
  • Noise: resident reviews specifically mention barely hearing same-floor or upstairs neighbors.
  • Budget: several 1BR configurations cluster right around the $2,500 line rather than dramatically above it.
  • Bottom line: likely the best “established luxury building” comparison against the newer Couture.

Ovation 309

Downtown Madison

Benchmark

Why it made the list: The Madison version of the true-luxury test: excellent construction plus actual front-desk/concierge service. It is worth seeing once even if downtown Madison ultimately feels too city-like.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
1BR: currently starts around $1,795, with many examples around $2,000–$2,470. 2BR: starts around $2,459, but many 2BRs are closer to $2,850–$3,000.
Best budget fit: 1BR. A 2BR can occasionally touch the $2,500 ceiling, but most are over it.
  • Stories: 14 stories above grade plus 2 below-grade parking levels — 16 structural levels total.
  • Construction/material: all 16 levels are post-tensioned concrete framing — one of the best construction profiles on this list for the “I do not want to hear upstairs neighbors” test.
  • Parking: heated, covered, controlled-access resident garage on underground levels P1 and P2; separate visitor/public parking is available on the first floor.
  • Service: real concierge/front-desk setup, controlled guest access and dedicated package handling.
  • Noise: resident comments repeatedly describe the building as very quiet/solid concrete.
  • Location: much closer to Beaver Dam than downtown Milwaukee while still offering a true high-rise-luxury comparison.
  • Bottom line: strong value if a 1BR works; potentially the most practical full-service tower geographically.

Heyday Sun Prairie

Sun Prairie

Must tour

Why it made the list: Heyday offers a very different version of upscale renting: single-story homes with private entrances and two-car garages, but with one professional leasing/maintenance operation handling the entire community. It removes the biggest apartment-noise concern — someone living above you — without requiring homeownership.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
1BR: about $1,820 (832 sf) or $2,215 (1,076 sf). 2BR: about $2,435 (1,344 sf) or $2,595 (1,457 sf). 3BR: pricing currently by inquiry.
Best budget fit: either 1BR and the smaller 2BR. The attached/private two-car garage is part of the housing concept rather than a separate luxury-tower add-on.
  • Stories: single-story ranch-style homes — no stairs inside the home and nobody living above or below.
  • Construction/material: wood-frame construction on slab-on-grade foundations. Although it is not concrete, the one-story design eliminates floor/ceiling impact noise from another household.
  • Parking: private direct-access 2-car garage with each home plus driveway/apron space; the property advertises private parking for up to four cars and EV-charging capability in each garage.
  • Management: professionally operated rental community with centralized leasing and maintenance, including 24-hour emergency maintenance.
  • Deliveries: each residence has its own address, so normal packages and food deliveries go directly to the home.
  • Setting: approximately 41 acres with walking paths and open space; this should feel substantially less urban than living in downtown Madison.
  • Distance to family: by far the closest option on this list to Beaver Dam — roughly half an hour by the fastest route.
Why pick this oneChoose Heyday if quiet, easy parking, proximity to Beaver Dam and low-hassle suburban living matter more than having a concierge or high-rise amenities. It is also one of the strongest options for staying under $2,500.
Why not pick this oneIt is not “hotel luxury.” There is no 24/7 concierge, the construction is wood-frame rather than concrete, and some larger layouts move above the preferred budget. The tour should also confirm how much side-wall noise carries between neighboring homes.

Residences at Oak View

Oak Creek

Lifestyle test

Why it made the list: These are actual detached single-family rental homes inside one professionally managed community. It is the clearest way to test whether renting a maintenance-free house feels better than renting even a very nice apartment.

Exterior
Interior
Planning price
2BR floor plans have recently advertised around $2,500–$2,550. 3BRs are roughly $3,100–$3,400. There are no 1BR layouts.
Current availability may skew toward larger homes, but the community does have 2BR plans. For an April 2027 move, the general 2BR price level matters more than today's exact vacancy list.
  • Stories: detached 1- and 2-story single-family rental homes, depending on floor plan.
  • Construction/material: detached-home construction with zero shared walls. A reliable public source specifying the exact structural framing material has not been found, so that is worth asking during the tour.
  • Parking: attached garage plus private/surface parking; the property advertises no separate parking or storage fees.
  • Privacy: no upstairs neighbor, no downstairs neighbor and no shared walls at all.
  • Management: professionally managed community rather than an individually owned rental house or small landlord arrangement.
  • Amenities: heated pool, clubhouse, gym, business center, pickleball/basketball, dog park and dog wash.
  • Guest option: a furnished guest home is available for visiting friends or family.
Why pick this oneChoose Oak View if maximum privacy is the priority. A detached rental gives the feel and quiet of a house while keeping professional maintenance, community amenities and no homeowner responsibilities.
Why not pick this oneThere is no 1BR option, 2BR pricing sits right at the budget ceiling, 3BRs are well above it, and Oak Creek is one of the farthest options from Beaver Dam. It also lacks the concierge/service experience of the luxury towers.

The Carillon at Grafton

Grafton · opening expected 2027

Future tour

Why it made the list: Carillon is a small build-to-rent community designed to feel more like private homes than apartments. The combination of attached garages, private yards, professional management and a planned 2027 opening makes it particularly relevant to an April 2027 move.

Exterior rendering
Interior rendering
Planning price
Final leasing prices have not been posted. During the approval process, the developer discussed a target of roughly $1.80–$2.30 per sq. ft. for homes of about 1,250–1,850 sf.
At that early planning range, a 1,250-sf home would be roughly $2,250–$2,875 per month and a 1,850-sf home roughly $3,330–$4,255. These are estimates from development-stage figures, NOT announced rents.
  • Stories: mix of single-story living and multi-level layouts with first-floor primary suites; only 34 homes across 10 buildings.
  • Construction/material: exact structural framing has not been clearly published. Exterior approvals allow LP engineered-wood or Hardie fiber-cement siding with stone-veneer accents on selected elevations.
  • Parking: every home is planned with an attached 1- or 2-car garage plus driveway/apron parking.
  • Timing: first occupancy is expected around spring 2027, closely matching the current lease-end timing.
  • Outdoor space: each home is planned with a private enclosed yard maintained by the community.
  • Scale: boutique-sized development rather than a large apartment complex, which may make it feel more residential and less institutional.
Why pick this oneChoose Carillon if the finished product delivers house-like privacy at the lower end of the projected rent range. Grafton, a private yard and an attached garage could be a strong long-term rental setup without buying yet.
Why not pick this oneIt is still under development, final rents are unknown, the exact structural framing is not yet clear, and pricing could easily land above $2,500. It should stay on the list, but only real leasing prices and a model-home tour can settle it.
